# Code of conduct

NuclearLang follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies to every space of the project: the repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, the
chat, and events where you represent the project.

## In short

- Be welcoming, kind and patient, in English, Spanish or any language the conversation is in.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, or unwelcome attention.
- Accept the maintainers' decisions on what is enforced; ask for a review of a decision in private.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainers at the contact email of the organization's profile
([github.com/nuclearlang](https://github.com/nuclearlang)) with what happened, where and when (links or screenshots
help). Reports are read by the maintainers only and kept confidential. You will get an answer within a week.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a correction, a warning, a
temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on the harm and whether it repeats.
